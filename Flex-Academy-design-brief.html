Flex Academy landing page design brief Sep 26, 2026 · @Someone Design assignment for Product / Brand Designer candidates: a landing page for Flex Academy, timeboxed to 6 to 8 hours over 7 days, unpaid and scoped to fit that time. Taking it on is your choice, not an obligation: you are free to accept or decline. It is the way we assess the candidates we want to hire. Why this assignment We are launching a new company and this page is its front door. We want to see how you think about conversion, brand and craft when you have real constraints and real assets. Treat it as your first week on the job, not a portfolio piece. • Role: Product / Brand Designer (landing pages and marketing sites) • Timebox: 6 to 8 hours of your time, spread over up to 7 days • Compensation: unpaid assessment, scoped to fit the timebox. We do not expect or reward more hours than that. Depth of thinking beats volume of screens. Context The Flex (theflex.global) operates fully furnished, move-in-ready apartments for short and long stays in London, Paris, Lisbon, Berlin, Algiers, Oran and Constantine, with Milan and Cairo coming. It serves travellers, relocating professionals and over 150 corporate housing partners. Tagline: "Flexible Living, Made Easy." Base360 (base360.ai) is the property management software the founders built to run The Flex, now sold to other short-term rental (STR) operators: channel manager across 130+ platforms, AI pricing, unified guest inbox, cleaning and maintenance, CRM, finance and HR. Tagline: "Run your Short Term Rentals on AI autopilot." Primary CTA: Book a demo. Both were founded by Raouf Yousfi and Michael Buggy. Flex Academy (new) helps people start and grow their own short-term rental company, using the playbook, the systems and the software the founders used to build The Flex. It targets all English-speaking countries and has two offers: • Learn how to launch (entry price, X): for newcomers starting their first STR business. This is the bigger market. It is sold through a free webinar that teaches, pitches the programme and closes. • Learn how to scale (premium price, Y, higher than X): for operators who already run units and want to grow into a real company. It is sold through a strategy call with the team. Final prices are not set: use X and Y as labelled placeholders. Audience Design for these two people, one per offer. Say in your Loom how you split the two paths and which one leads. 1. The 9-to-5 leaver (Launch offer): employed, wants to quit their job and build an income they own. Has no units yet or just one, and needs the playbook to start safely. This is the bigger market. 2. The early operator (Scale offer): already started, runs a few units, and wants to grow into a real company. Stuck on manual work, thin margins and no time to grow. They live across English-speaking countries (UK, US, Canada, Australia and others). They are ambitious, busy, sceptical of "guru" courses, and comparing us against YouTube, cheap online courses and expensive masterminds. The job of the page Primary conversions, one per offer: • Launch: "Register for the free webinar." A short sign-up (name, email, country), then a confirmation with the date and an add-to-calendar option. • Scale: "Book a free strategy call." A 20 to 30 minute call with the team, qualified by a short form (units today, target, city, budget band), then a calendar step and confirmation. Design the moment of commitment for both: the CTA, the form or the hand-off to a calendar, and the confirmation state. Secondary conversion: join the waitlist or get the free "STR scaling checklist" by email, for visitors who are not ready to talk. Success looks like: a visitor understands in 5 seconds who this is for and what they get, trusts us within 30 seconds, and can act at any point of the scroll without hunting. Scope Required: • Desktop landing page, 1440 px wide, full scroll. One coherent page, not a site. • Mobile, 390 px wide: the hero and one more section of your choice (the one where you think mobile changes the design the most). • The CTA flows for both offers (webinar registration and strategy call booking): button state, form or calendar step, confirmation. Static frames are fine. • A 1-page design notes frame in Figma (or the Loom alone if you prefer talking): audience choice, page structure, 3 key decisions. Optional, only inside the timebox: • Micro-interaction or motion concept for the hero (a few frames or a prototype). • A dark mode or an alternative art direction for one section. Out of scope: logo redesign, full brand system, copy for every section, development. Assets you can use • Live sites: theflex.global and base360.ai (logos, colours, type, photography, product screenshots, testimonials, city list). You may extend both brands; Flex Academy should feel like a sibling, not a copy.
